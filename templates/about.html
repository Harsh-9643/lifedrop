About LifeDrop

A community-driven platform connecting blood donors with those in urgent need.

Our Mission

LifeDrop was born from a simple but powerful idea: no one should die because they couldn't find blood in time. We bridge the gap between willing donors and patients in need — creating a seamless network of life-savers across India.

Whether it's a planned surgery or a sudden accident, blood is irreplaceable. No machine can make it. Only humans can give it. That's why every registered donor on our platform is a true hero.

Who Can Donate?

✅ You Can Donate If:

  • You are between 18–65 years of age
  • You weigh at least 50 kg
  • Your hemoglobin is ≥12.5 g/dL
  • You are in good general health
  • It has been 3 months since your last donation
  • You have not taken antibiotics in the last 48 hours

❌ You Cannot Donate If:

  • You have had a cold or flu in the last 2 weeks
  • You are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • You have had a tattoo or piercing in the last 6 months
  • You have been diagnosed with HIV, Hepatitis B/C
  • You have undergone major surgery recently
  • You are under certain medications
